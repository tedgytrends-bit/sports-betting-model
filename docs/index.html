TRANSPARENCY / DATA & LIMITATIONS

Model methodology.

How our single public Injury projection is built, what the research models do, and how the official record is preserved.

01 / THE FOUNDATION

Baseline

Our established 24-feature Baseline remains in research and historical performance tracking. It estimates team scores using historical efficiency and performance features. The published Baseline is the unchanged standard version—not a short-term-weighting experiment or six-game scoring-average fallback.

02 / PLAYER AVAILABILITY

Injury-adjusted

The single public QB-aware Injury model combines non-QB injury inputs with the projected starter's historical EPA per dropback relative to his team's recent QB performance. Small samples are regularized; the estimated QB effect is capped at five points per team and adjusts the projected margin while preserving the non-QB modeled total. A provisional QB listing is not a confirmed starter.

03 / TIMESTAMPS & AUDIT

Frozen model and market data

Predictions are saved in dated GitHub files rather than recalculated on every website visit. Week 9's first projections remain archived; the final Injury release for Sunday's and Monday's 14 games is separately timestamped. Bovada prices are separately archived from user-provided screenshots. Reference timestamps may be ingestion times or approximate screenshot times, as documented in each source row; neither guarantees the sportsbook's quote time or an executable market price. Updating the market doesn't recalculate the models. Open any game for its sources.

04 / RESULTS

Published versus retrospective

The public prospective record begins with the full 14-game Week 8 slate. Weeks 1–7 were reconstructed historically, not published before kickoff. QB EPA backtest improvements were assessed retrospectively and are not independently validated evidence of future wins or profitability. Official frozen records are preserved when a newer model version is published.

Source files & research disclosures

Tedgy Trends is a public NFL research dashboard, not betting advice. The site reads committed data from GitHub; it does not train models or call a live sportsbook. Model leans indicate projected differences from the archived market, not guaranteed edges. Injury report availability is not proof that every absence was correctly recognized, and most Week 9 quarterback listings are provisional unless otherwise source-verified.
