TRANSPARENCY / DATA & LIMITATIONS

About the models.

How the projections work, what the numbers mean, and how results are tracked.

01 / FOUNDATION

Baseline

Projects each team’s score using efficiency and performance data. It is shown alongside Injury projections for comparison.

02 / PLAYER AVAILABILITY

Injury model

Adds team injury inputs and a QB performance adjustment. Unverified starters and inexperienced replacements can make these estimates unreliable.

03 / THE MARKET

ATS and totals

Each projection is compared with a saved Bovada spread and total. A lean shows the direction of the difference; it is not a guarantee or a recommended wager.

04 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Published results

The public record starts in Week 8. Forecasts and Bovada lines are archived before kickoff; results update when games are final. Research comparisons remain separate from the original predictions.

All scores and sportsbook lines are archived snapshots, not live odds. Model leans are research indicators—not betting advice.
