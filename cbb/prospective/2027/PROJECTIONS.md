## How to read this board

- These are **latest** projections, not frozen records. Season wins, losses and ROI are graded against the original timestamped pretip frozen forecasts linked above.
- Market lines are the sportsbook numbers observed **at the capture timestamp**, not live quotes.
- When there is no market spread or total, a corresponding ATS or total lean cannot be determined. Such games are kept in the full JSON projection board.
- All five models are experimental. No model has established reliable profitability across all historical directional predictions. This is research, not betting advice.
- Captures are scheduled at 13:05 UTC each day November–April; GitHub Actions can start late.
